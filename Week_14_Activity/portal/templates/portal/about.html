About the CCS Student Portal

Portal Purpose

The CCS Student Portal was developed to streamline communication between the College of Computer Studies and its student body. Instead of searching across multiple bulletin boards or social media pages, students can access verified academic updates and resources in one unified web application.

Intended Users

This portal is designed primarily for undergraduate Computer Science and Information Technology students, as well as department faculty members and academic advisers who share course materials and notices.

Accessibility Features Implemented

  • High-Contrast Color Palette: Text and background combinations exceed WCAG AA contrast standards (dark slate #1e293b on off-white #f8fafc, and crisp white on deep navy #163a54).
  • Keyboard-Friendly Navigation & Focus Indicators: Includes a hidden "Skip to main content" link for keyboard users and high-visibility focus outlines (#ffdb70) on all interactive links.
  • Semantic HTML & Descriptive Alt Text: Uses semantic landmark elements (header, nav, main, footer) and meaningful alt attributes for screen readers.
